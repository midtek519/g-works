RECOMMENDATION / CONVERSATION NOTES

A person, their learning, their plans.

Prompts for a conversation, not a standard letter or a checklist to complete.

Name
Date
Application / opportunity
Relationship / course / semester

1. Beyond the subject content

What is the most important thing you learned from the class that was not subject content?

2. Learning and education

How has your approach to learning changed? What do you think makes education meaningful?

3. Feedback, difficulty, and revision

Tell me about a setback or a piece of feedback. How did you respond, and what changed?

4. Contribution to others

How did you contribute to the class or help someone else learn? Give a specific example.
